Important medical and technical disclaimer

  • This site is for educational and informational use only; it is not a diagnostic device and is not medical advice.
  • Consumer SNP arrays assay a limited subset of the genome and can miss many clinically relevant variants.
  • Rare-variant calls from SNP chips and direct-to-consumer exports can be inaccurate; clinically significant findings require confirmatory testing.
  • The bundled DuckGeneSnap asset set is intentionally small. Rebuild production bundles from authoritative ClinVar/GWAS/PharmGKB/dbSNP sources with provenance.
